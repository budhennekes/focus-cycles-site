Focus Cycles for Mac

A timer with a plan.

Choose a target, focus on the next action, and review what happened. A calm work rhythm for writing, studying, and the task you keep putting off.

Coming to the Mac App Store for Apple silicon Macs.

Give the work a beginning and an ending.

01

Plan the next step.

Name what you want to accomplish and how you will begin. A small first action gives the session somewhere to go.

02

Stay with one target.

Keep your target beside the countdown. Pause when you need to, and take a break between cycles.

03

Keep the useful part.

Write down what happened. Save the reflection so the next session does not have to start from scratch.

Your session notes stay on your Mac.

Plans, reviews, and history are stored locally. The core timer and local sounds work offline. Built-in online scenery needs an internet connection. Read the privacy policy for the details.
