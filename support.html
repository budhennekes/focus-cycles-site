Focus Cycles · Mac

A little help, then back to work.

For Focus Cycles support or privacy questions, email bud@aboundlessworld.com.

Tell us what happened

Include the app version, your macOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help. Remove personal information first. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Plan → Focus → Review

Choose your cycles and durations. Set a target, focus on the next action, then review what worked. Your session records stay on your Mac unless you export and share them.

Window controls

Use the yellow window button or ⌘M to minimize. During a timer, use ⌘⇧M for Compact mode. Closing the window keeps the app running in the menu bar. Use ⌘Q to quit.

Recovery and saved history

If a session needs saving, use Retry before starting another. If the app reopens with a recovery prompt, choose whether to resume. Do not delete app data while trying to recover a session.

Offline use

The timer and local session records work without internet. Online scenery may be unavailable. Local sounds do not stream audio.
